# About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the **Archimedes Unit of the Athena Research Center**. I work on trustworthy and efficient machine learning, combining theoretical analysis, algorithm development, and empirical evaluation.

My research focuses on **memorization and privacy**, **language-model evaluation**, and **efficient multimodal learning**. I earned my Ph.D. at Purdue University in 2024, working in the Neuro(nano) Research Laboratory with Professor Kaushik Roy.

## Education

- **Ph.D., Electrical and Computer Engineering** — Purdue University, 2024. Advisor: Professor Kaushik Roy.
- **M.Sc., Computer Science** — University of Thessaly, 2017. With distinction; valedictorian.
- **B.Sc., Mathematics** — National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, 2016. Applied Mathematics specialization.

## Research service & mentorship

- **Research service:** TMLR Action Editor; reviewer for NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, TMLR, and other machine learning venues.
- **Research mentorship:** co-supervised projects on differentially private mental-health classification and cross-modal generative augmentation for biological classification, resulting in publications in Frontiers in Digital Health (2025) and TMLR (2026).
- **Invited talks:** University of Manchester and Athena Research and Innovation Center (2025).

## Mentoring & outreach

### Greek delegation at IOAI 2026

In August 2026, I joined the Greek delegation as an **observer** at the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence in Astana, Kazakhstan. I delivered preparatory lectures for the Greek team and accompanied the students throughout the competition and its activities; the four students earned **two silver and two bronze medals**.

I was happy to support the team as they tackled challenging AI problems and connected with peers from around the world. Thank you to the Archimedes Research Unit for making this participation possible.
